## Goal

Write `gtm/assets/maintainer-email.md`: a first email to the maintainer of an open-source project
asking whether they would list a few tasks for Swarm volunteers. The reader is busy and has seen
many low-quality machine-written pull requests. The email has to earn a reply in its first two
sentences and must be easy to say no to.

## Rules

- Frontmatter: `channel: outreach-email` and a `title`.
- Read `gtm/channels/outreach-email.json` for length and required headings. Put the subject line
  under a `## Subject` heading and the body under `## Body`.
- Use `[name]` and `[project]` as the only placeholders. Do not flatter the project.
- Say what the maintainer has to do, what they control, and one honest limit. Link the adoption
  guide at `docs/ADOPTING.md` in the repository.
- Every factual statement must come from `gtm/facts.json`.
