## Goal

Write `gtm/assets/newsletter-blurb.md`: one paragraph that the editor of a developer newsletter
can paste unchanged. It says what Swarm is, the three steps, and the one limit a reader most needs
to know, then gives the repository link from `gtm/facts.json`.

## Rules

- Frontmatter: `channel: blurb` and a `title`.
- Read `gtm/channels/blurb.json` for length. One paragraph, no headings, no lists.
- Third person, present tense. No superlatives and no exclamation marks.
- Every factual statement must come from `gtm/facts.json`.
